About

Description

Fernando Botero Angulo was a Colombian figurative artist and sculptor. His signature style, also known as "Boterismo", depicts people and figures in large, exaggerated volume, which can represent political criticism or humor, depending on the piece. Wikipedia
Born: April 19, 1932, Medellín
Died: September 15, 2023 (age 91 years), Monaco, Monaco
Periods: Contemporary art, Modern art
Spouse: Sophia Vari (m. 1978–2023), Cecilia Zambrano (m. 1964–1975), Gloria Zea (m. 1955–1960)
Education: Liceo Antioqueño - Sede Principal (1950) · See more
Children: Fernando Botero Zea, Lina Botero, Juan Carlos Botero, Pedro Botero Zambrano
